Healthy Lifestyle

Armed with scientific and medical facts to fuel and inspire your journey toward healthy living.

“Exercise, hydration, sleep.”

Choices

What are the essential choices for healthy living?

You are what you eat
A balanced diet rich in nutrients from a variety of foods supports overall health and reduces the risk of chronic disease.
Water
Staying well hydrated is essential for maintaining bodily functions, sharpening cognitive performance, and supporting overall well-being.
Sleep
Adequate sleep is crucial for physical recovery, mental clarity, and a healthy metabolism.
Exercise & balance
Regular physical activity, combined with rest and balance in life, builds strength, supports heart health, and promotes mental well-being.

Outcomes

  • Understand lean eating and a plant-forward diet
  • See clearly what vegan and vegetarian eating truly entails
  • Know the difference between eating for weight loss and eating for performance
  • Weigh the benefits of healthy aging and disease prevention against relying on medication

How it works

From welcome to the last bite

  1. Reach out

    Tell us about your group and a few dates that work. We will plan the experience together.

  2. Get the recipes

    Before class you will receive a welcome note, the recipes, and everything you need to know about arrival.

  3. Share any dietary needs

    Let us know about allergies or restrictions in advance so everyone can enjoy the meal safely.

  4. Cook, then gather

    Arrive, meet Stacy and Noah, pair up, and cook. Then sit down together for the meal you created.

Questions

Do I need any cooking experience?

Not at all. Every recipe is broken down step by step, from mise en place to plating, and we are right there with you the whole time.

Who is the experience for?

Groups of friends, families, teams, and anyone curious about plant-forward cooking. It is a wonderful setting for celebrations and team building.

Is everything vegan?

Yes. Every dish is plant-based, and we love showing skeptics just how flavorful plants can be.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Please tell us about any allergies or dietary restrictions when you book, and we will plan the menu around them.

Where do classes take place?

In our home kitchen in San Francisco. The address and arrival details are shared with your group once your date is confirmed.
